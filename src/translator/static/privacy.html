What the service does with your text

When you press Generate or a tweak button, the text you entered (what they wrote, what you want to say, and the previous reply for a tweak) is sent to an AI model provider, Anthropic (Claude) or, for some styles, OpenAI, to write the reply. It passes through our own gateway on the way. The providers process it under their API terms, which do not allow using it to train their models. We do not read your texts and do not use them for anything else.

What is stored

  • Without an account: nothing about your texts is stored on our server. Your history and preferences stay on your device (in the browser's local storage, or in the Android app's private storage) until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • With an account: your email address and the user ID from sign-in, and your history (your inputs and the replies, up to the latest 200 items) unless you tick "Don't save this one". It stays until you delete it or your account.
  • For every use: the number of AI tokens used and their cost per day and style, without any link to you, so we can keep spending under a daily limit.
  • Server logs: IP address, time and address of each request, for running and protecting the service. They are rotated automatically and kept only for a short time.
  • A session cookie (or the app's equivalent) keeps you verified or signed in. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Services we use

  • Anthropic and OpenAI: writing the replies (see above).
  • Clerk: sign-in with Google. Clerk and Google receive your sign-in details (name, email, profile picture) under their own privacy policies.
  • Cloudflare: network delivery and Turnstile, the check that you are not a bot. Cloudflare processes your IP address and technical data about your browser or device.
  • Amazon Web Services: the server that runs the service, located in the United States.

Some of these companies process data outside the EU, in particular in the United States, on the basis of the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

Legal basis

Processing your texts and your account is necessary to provide the service you ask for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Bot protection, spending limits and logs are based on our legitimate interest in running the service securely and affordably (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Deleting your data

You can delete single history items or all of them in the app or on the website. To delete your account and everything stored with it, use "Delete account" in the app's account menu or the account deletion page, or email us. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict and port your data and to object to its processing. Write to the email address above. You can also complain to the Austrian data protection authority (Datenschutzbehörde, dsb.gv.at).

Children

The service is not directed at children under 14.
